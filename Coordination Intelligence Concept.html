Coordination Intelligence · Concept experience

OR 4 was ready at 06:50. Why did the first case start at 07:52?

Coordination Intelligence uses ceiling sensors in the OR suite to spot a delay while it's still forming, suggest why from what the rooms already show, and draft the next step for the charge nurse to approve. No cameras in patient spaces.

    05 · How it works

    Five steps, every morning

    01

    Sense

    Ceiling sensors pick up room status and the movement of people and equipment. No cameras in patient spaces.

    02

    Notice

    The system flags a case that's falling behind before the start time passes.

    03

    Explain

    It pulls the relevant signals together and suggests a likely cause, with a confidence level.

    04

    Act

    It drafts the next step and assigns an owner and due time. Staff approve or edit it, and follow-up is automatic.

    05

    Learn

    The outcome is logged. Recurring causes show up in the weekly OR review, and each case improves the model.

    06 · How we'll know

    What the pilot has to show

    Proposed measures for a 5 to 10 OR pilot. Targets are set with the hospital against a baseline before go-live.

    First cases starting on time

    Proposed: late starts cut by a third

    Measured against the hospital's own baseline period.

    Turnover time between cases

    Proposed: median down 5+ minutes

    From patient out to next patient in.

    Late starts with a known cause

    Proposed: every one

    Logged with an owner, not coded after the fact.

    Drafted steps approved as written

    Proposed: a clear majority

    Tells us whether suggestions are good enough to trust.

    Time spent finding equipment

    Proposed: measurably lower

    Trays, beds, and devices located without calls or walks.

    The go-back test

    Proposed: a clear no at day 90

    Would the OR team want to go back to working without it?

    Assumptions we still have to prove

    • The sensors see enoughThe ceiling sensors can reliably tell room status, readiness, and equipment location apart in a working OR suite.
    • Suggestions are good enoughLikely causes and drafted steps are accurate enough that charge nurses approve them without heavy editing.
    • It fits the workflowAlerts reach staff inside the tools they already use, including the OR board and the EHR.
    • It beats what's thereIt catches delays earlier than the OR board and EHR timestamps already do, by enough to matter.
