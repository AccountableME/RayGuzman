The short version

Ray Guzman builds and governs businesses. Over a 25-year career he has taken companies from concept to exit, held executive roles inside one of the most complex enterprises ever built, and sat in the boardrooms of financial institutions, universities, and healthcare companies. That combination of founder's urgency and director's discipline is what makes him effective: he understands what management is actually dealing with, not just what the financials say.
