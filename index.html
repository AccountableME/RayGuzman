How Ray governs

Judgment, process, and the questions that matter

Ray's approach in the boardroom is consistent and unflashy: governance is not technical mastery, it is the discipline of asking the right questions, insisting on rigorous answers, and holding management accountable for both. He believes a director is judged on process, not just outcome, and he applies the same discipline to himself, asking each year whether he is still adding value to the board.

Organizations rise to the level of the questions being asked by their board directors.

Distilled from Ray's essays in Thoughts from within the Boardroom.

AI board governance

Governing AI without standing in its way

On AI, Ray's conviction is that directors do not have to master a technology to govern it. The board's job is a strong, frequently refreshed governance process, with AI governed as an extension of enterprise risk management rather than a standalone mystery.

"Our goal is to foster innovation, not hinder it."
Ray Guzman, in conversation with Odgers Berndtson
