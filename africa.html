Weights

Scores are weighted averages of 0–100 sub-scores (min-max normalized across the 54 countries). Missing dimensions are dropped and the rest reweighted, so low-coverage scores lean on fewer facts — anything under 80% coverage is flagged.

Method

Each dimension is a 0–100 sub-score, min-max normalized across the 54 countries and direction-aligned so higher is always better (Peace Index, Hunger Index, tax rate and cost-of-living index are inverted). The weighted score divides by the weight of the dimensions a country actually has, so missing data never counts as zero — but it does mean less evidence, hence the coverage flag. The "rubric" mode scores the 8 Phase-2 dimensions by documented judgment; "objective v2" rebuilds six of them from third-party indicators (residency pathways and single-entity holding remain rubric — no clean global index exists).
